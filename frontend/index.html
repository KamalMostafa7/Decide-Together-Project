What is Decide Together?

A platform that connects citizens with their communities to report, track, and resolve local issues across Egypt.

Our Goal

To create a transparent, efficient, and democratic platform that empowers Egyptian citizens to actively participate in improving their communities. We bridge the gap between citizens and local authorities, ensuring every voice is heard and every issue is addressed.
